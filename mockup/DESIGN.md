## Brand & Style
This design system establishes an ultra-refined, editorial health philosophy bridging advanced diagnostics and clinical psychology. Combining Swiss modernist structure with calm precision, the experience reflects the rigorous intellect of functional medicine without clinical sterility.

The personality balances medical authority, emotional stillness, and quiet confidence. Designed for patients navigating intricate health landscapes and discerning medical practitioners, the aesthetic pairs high-contrast monochrome foundations with soft, restorative natural tones. The layout language relies on expansive white and bone surfaces, sculptural grotesque headlines, stark deep obsidian chapters, and disciplined hairline architectural borders.

## Colors
The palette adopts an intentional high-contrast rhythm inspired by physical monographs. Foundations are built upon two primary canvas states: pure clinical light (`#FFFFFF` anchored with `#F8F7F4` bone) and deep, cinematic void blocks (`#0A0B0A`).

Accentuation is restrained:
- **Primary Ink (`#0F1110`)**: Deep charcoal-black delivering maximum contrast against bone surfaces without the optical vibration of synthetic pure black.
- **Clinical Sage (`#4D6357` & `#8FA396`)**: Evokes psychological restoration and biological balance. Used sparingly for status indicators, active states, key tags, and subtle hover halos.
- **Bone & Sand (`#F8F7F4`, `#D6C7B2`)**: Warm neutral fields that soften clinical austerity, creating human warmth across card surfaces and secondary editorial blocks.
- **Hairline Dividers (`#E2E0D9` and `#232624`)**: Thin, architectural rule lines defining column separations and data matrices.

## Typography
Typography is unified under Hanken Grotesk—an immaculate, contemporary grotesque with tight aperture and refined horizontal proportions that channels mid-century modernist editorial spreads.

Hierarchy principles:
- **Punctuation and Em-Dashes**: Headlines feature prominent em-dashes (`—`) to create deliberate pauses between diagnostic concepts.
- **Metric Figures**: Counters rely on lean, large tabular numerals positioned directly alongside micro-superscripts or unit indicators.
- **Editorial Category Indexes**: Sub-clinical marker lists, conditions, and biomarkers use tight, rhythmic lines (`index-item`) arranged in dense multi-column arrays.
- **Casing**: All titles and headlines maintain clean sentence or title casing. Micro-labels and tags employ subtle tracking (`+0.02em` to `+0.06em`) for surgical legibility.

## Layout & Spacing
The layout relies on a strict 12-column architectural grid with structural hairline borders subdividing content compartments. 

- **Desktop (1024px+)**: 12 columns with 32px gutters and 48px to 64px outer page margins. Section padding scales generously (`space-4xl` to `space-5xl`), giving each medical thesis breathing room.
- **Tablet (768px - 1023px)**: 8 columns with 24px gutters and 32px margins. 3- and 6-column marker arrays compress gracefully to 2 columns.
- **Mobile (< 768px)**: 4 columns with 16px gutters and 20px margins. Multi-column index modules collapse into stacked accordion lists or horizontal carousels with hairline separators.

Content alternates rhythmically between luminous off-white (`#F8F7F4`) editorial spreads and edge-to-edge dark immersive portals (`#0A0B0A`).

## Elevation & Depth
This design system renounces drop shadows in favor of flat planar rigor, tonal layering, and hairline grid containment:

- **Borders over Shadows**: Spatial separation is achieved exclusively via 1px crisp borders (`#E2E0D9` on light surfaces; `#232624` on dark surfaces). Floating elevations and blurry ambient drop shadows are strictly avoided.
- **Stacked Planar Tone**: Elevated layers (modals, slide-overs, active floating selectors) use solid surfaces (`#FFFFFF` over `#F8F7F4`, or `#141715` over `#0A0B0A`) framed with crisp 1px borders.
- **Frosted Translucency**: Glassmorphism is reserved for floating fixed navigation and pill triggers, configured as `backdrop-filter: blur(16px)` with 85% opacity light/dark fills and semi-transparent perimeter borders.

## Shapes
A dual geometry governs the interface:
- **Pill Primitives (`9999px`)**: Interactive triggers, status badges, counter chips, navigation bars, and CTA buttons are shaped as exact mathematical capsules.
- **Architectural Cards (`0px` to `8px`)**: Large content containers, data grids, image masks, and index modules remain clean and sharp with minimal or no corner rounding, establishing a scientific, clinical baseline that contrasts with the ergonomic pill buttons.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary Pill Action**: A solid dark capsule (`#0F1110` background, `#FFFFFF` text) featuring a circular inner icon disc at the trailing edge. Inside the disc rests a fine rightward directional arrow (`→`). Padding is `10px 10px 10px 24px`.
- **Secondary / Ghost Pill**: Transparent background with a 1px border (`#0F1110` or `#E2E0D9`), pairing clean text with an optional arrow badge.
- **Inverse Action**: For dark canvas sections, the pill reverses to pure white fill (`#FFFFFF`) with dark obsidian text and dark trailing icon badge.

### Pill Badges & Chips
- Restrained capsules featuring 4px horizontal padding, 20px height, with subtle sage or bone fills (`#E8ECE9` or `#EEEDE8`) and dark sage or graphite typography (`#4D6357`). Used to denote biomarker counts, system categories, and status confirmations.

### Metric Counters
- Composed of large display figures (`48px` Hanken Grotesk, 400 weight) anchored with top-right or inline micro-labels. Metric blocks feature top hairline dividers and generous vertical spacing.

### Accordion & Multi-Column Indexes
- **Specialty Indexes**: Clean 3-to-6 column layouts displaying biological categories (e.g., "Gut, Digestion & Microbiome", "Hormones, Fertility & Reproduction"). Each column starts with an editorial headline followed by an alphabetical vertical list of conditions separated by delicate spacing.
- **FAQ / Question Rows**: Full-width bordered horizontal strips with headline-sm text and a minimal trailing plus/cross toggle (`+` / `×`).

### Cards & Surfaces
- Structural, rectangular panels with 1px border definition, zero ambient shadow, and pure `#FFFFFF` or `#F8F7F4` backgrounds. Padding scales uniformly from 24px to 40px depending on screen viewport.

### Input Fields & Selectors
- Pill-shaped or clean underline-style inputs with `body-base` sizing, muted placeholder text (`#767975`), and active focus states signaled via sharp sage border transitions without thick outer rings.
